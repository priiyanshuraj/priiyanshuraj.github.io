### Qaint is a research-driven publication on product, growth, and revenue systems.
#### It examines how startups actually scale — beyond surface-level tactics and trend-driven advice. Here, product strategy is analyzed through a commercial lens. Growth is treated as a system, not a hack.
#### Sales is understood as structured positioning, not persuasion. From Indian SaaS deep dives to go-to-market breakdowns and experimentation frameworks, Qaint focuses on applied insight — the kind founders, operators, and revenue teams can execute on.The mission is simple: Build clarity around how modern technology businesses grow.
