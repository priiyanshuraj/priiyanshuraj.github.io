This is a small corner of the internet where I think out loud. I write about whatever is occupying my mind at the moment, curiosities, passing thoughts, half-finished ideas, and things I don’t want to forget. There is no fixed theme here, just an honest record of what I’m noticing, questioning, and learning.

I don’t claim to have answers. Most of these essays are attempts to understand myself better, to make sense of the world around me, and to put vague feelings into clear sentences. If something you read here makes you pause, disagree, or feel a little less alone in your own confusion, then this page has done its job.
